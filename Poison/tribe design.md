# Cymanti - Poison
### General idea
This tribe is designed aound the poison mechanics that Cymanti is already known for. This also comes with the expectation that the tribe is designed to be very defensive oriented, with many of the more aggressive units removed from the tribe. I'm unsure if I want this tribe to replace Cymanti directly or be a tribe adjacent to it
### Identifying mechanics and mechanical rules
1. Cymanti will keep Raychi and living island as their primary naval units, but not boomchi. I also plan on making phychi be unlocked from naval techs, and providing a different poison archer on archery
2. They will also keep algae instead of the embark system. Some work will be needed to get this system to play nicely with roads (as I initally intend on allowing Poison Tribe to use roads as their movement mechanic)
3. Because units are designed to be defensive in nature. and especially because of the poison ability, Units should ideally not have attack stats (against non poisoned units) greater than what they are replacing. Equal stats are fine. There are some exceptions to this rule:
    - Units that are not directly replacing base units don't need to follow this rule in order to allow for a flexibility in design
    - Units that have some other downside to make up for their increased stats
    - Units specifically designed to be cleanup units
4. Units like the mantis are allowed to continue to exist, but will lose the crawl ability in order to better function with mechanics like roads. This does however mean that mantis will \*not\* be a unique unit in any manner beyond its sprite
5. The super unit will be replaced, though I'm unsure what it should be replaced with
6. Their mechanics should ideally avoid movement nerfs to the enemy in any way. This is to avoid slowing the game down too much, espiecailly as this tribe is supposed to be defensive first
