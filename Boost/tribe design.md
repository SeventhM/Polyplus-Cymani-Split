# Cymanti - Boost
## NOTE: THIS TRIBE DOES NOT HAVE A NAME YET
### General idea
This tribe is designed aound the making the boost mechanic its own functioning identity. This should lead to a very aggressive tribe with very few good defensive options
### Identifying mechanics and mechanical rules
1. The boost mechanic will replace roads as a movement mechanic, and mycelium will be give to the Boost Tribe to handle city connections
2. Notably, the boost mechanic will be an updated take on it. It will have the +0.5 attack that Cymanti used to have, but will only go away on attack, not when attacked or when doing other actions. These changes should make the boost mechanic more consistent than the current version of it
3. In order to make making shamans less of a bother, this tribe will be given a replacement building for guilds that allows them to create units on top of the pop the guilds themselves make
4. In order to balance that out, most of their units should have poor defensive capibilities. Even the defender is being replaced in this current proposal to be more aggressive
5. The tribe has a secondary theme of explode units. The explosion ability is great as the unit dies on use, making it also an ability only useful offensively
6. the Explosion ability will not have any of its charateristics from the current version of Cymanti. It also will not be available after attacking
7. The tribe in its current state is expected to make use of the normal tribe embark naval system
8. It is not important that the tribe keeps centipedes as they are currently implemented. However, it would be ideal for them to have a super unit that is very similar (low hp, benefits from killing things, and can explode)
